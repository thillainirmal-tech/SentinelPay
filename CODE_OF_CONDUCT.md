# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must communicate respectfully, welcome constructive technical disagreement, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or disclosure of another person's private information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behaviour is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact listed on the GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in public.
